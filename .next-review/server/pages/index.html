01 / THE PROFILE

Gazi Md. Mozammel Haque brings a background in public administration and institutional development to his role as Project Director of Anondo Housing Society.

His professional journey connects public service with the practical work of managing land, strengthening infrastructure and supporting the welfare of communities. It is a career shaped by responsibility for both institutions and the people they serve.

He also serves as Joint Secretary of the Bangladesh Kabaddi Federation , extending his leadership into the sporting community.

From his roots in Cumilla to his studies at the University of Dhaka and service with Bangladesh Police, each chapter forms part of a continuing commitment to purposeful leadership.

02 / THE JOURNEY
ROOTS01

A foundation in service

Born in 1966 in Haripur, Meghna, Cumilla, into a family with a legacy of service. His father, Gazi Md. Safar Ali, was a freedom fighter and an organiser of the Liberation War; his mother was Saleha Begum.

EDUCATION02

University of Dhaka

Studied Applied Physics and Electronics at the University of Dhaka, developing an analytical foundation for a career in administration and public service.

PUBLIC SERVICE03

Bangladesh Police

Entered the Bangladesh Civil Service (Police) as an Assistant Superintendent of Police. His career includes service as an Additional Deputy Inspector General at Police Headquarters.

PROJECT LEADERSHIP04

Anondo Housing Society

As Project Director of Anondo Housing Society, he brings experience in administration, land management, infrastructure and welfare initiatives to project leadership.

SPORTS LEADERSHIP05

Bangladesh Kabaddi Federation

He serves as Joint Secretary of the Bangladesh Kabaddi Federation, extending his leadership into the sporting community.

PUBLIC SERVICE IN PRACTICE

Information systems

01

Land Information Bank

Bringing land records into a structured, accessible information system.

An initiative to consolidate information on Bangladesh Police land holdings across the country, supporting informed planning, record management and institutional oversight.

Asset management

02

Building & Infrastructure Records

A clearer picture of the assets that support an institution.

A framework for organising records of police buildings and facilities, helping make property information useful for maintenance, administration and future infrastructure planning.

Public interest

03

Land Protection & Recovery

Safeguarding public assets through responsible stewardship.

Contributions to correcting inaccurate land records and recovering land from unauthorised occupation, with a focus on protecting institutional property and the public interest.

Community welfare

04

Housing & Wellbeing

Supporting the people and families behind public service.

Responsibilities in housing initiatives for police personnel, their families and associated communities, connecting infrastructure planning with long-term welfare.
